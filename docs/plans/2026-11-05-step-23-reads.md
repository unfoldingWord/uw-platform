## Rulings

| # | Ruling | Why | Cost if wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| R23-1 | **A day is a UTC calendar day,** `YYYY-MM-DD` through `formatDate`; the 30 days end today and run oldest first, every day present, zero when nothing happened | Plan section 0 dates; a chart needs every day | A person far from UTC sees a run late in their evening on the next day |
| R23-2 | **Gate waits per day** is, for day D, the gate decisions recorded on D plus the items parked at a gate whose `held_since` is on or before D. The first counts waits that ended that day, the second waits still open; together they are the waits a person had in front of them that day, from the two sources design section 7 names | No table records when a past wait began; `held_since` exists only for a wait still open | A wait that began and ended on different past days counts only on the day it ended |
| R23-3 | **Each item's latest event** is one read for a list of items (`latestForItems`), so the Board's cards cost one query, not one per card | Design section 4.4: a card shows its last event | None |
| R23-4 | **The items the viewer holds** are the items whose `claimed_by` is the viewer's identity, not done, up to the view limit, by priority as the Board orders them (no column records when a claim was made); capped says so | Design section 7 | An agent's claims made on the person's behalf are not the person's; they show on the Board, not under My Items |
