Chad Initiative

{{modeNote}}

markdown at rest v37 · saved 2026-08-24 16:12 3 open suggestions 2 required policies

Need

Fourteen languages in the eastern cluster have no scripture in a form the local church can use, and the three partner organizations working there keep separate records that do not reconcile.

Idea

Run one church-centric cohort per cluster, with quarterly in-person check-ins with monthly remote check-ins and two in-person visits per year with monthly remote check-ins and two in-person visits per year, training local facilitators who can train the next cohort without us.

Strategy

Three projects run in sequence, each pulling from the parking lot only when the previous one clears its WIP limit:

  • Facilitator cohort — 12 trainers across 4 partner organizations
  • Open Bible Stories drafting — 14 languages, checked against the gateway language
  • Community checking and publishing to the open licence

Outcomes & expected evidence

Risks

Travel access to the eastern cluster is not guaranteed; the contingency is a remote-only cohort at reduced pace, costed in the work order. Records for this program are sensitivity: protected — engagement detail never reaches published pages or org-wide dashboards.
