uw-platform · design system v1 · proposal · 2026-11-24

Calm by default, clear when it matters.

One page of rules every new screen starts from. It keeps the tokens you already lock in packages/ui/tokens/ and adds the layer that was missing: how pieces combine, when things appear, and which page shape a screen starts from.

Five principles

01

A page, not a pile of cards

Content sits on one white surface. Groups are separated by space and a soft hairline. A bordered card is kept for an object you can open, move or act on: a queue item, a record, a figure.

02

Quiet until asked

Row actions, menus and handles appear on hover or keyboard focus. On touch screens they are always shown. Nothing is reachable only by hover.

03

One loud thing per view

At most one Inspire-filled primary button per view. Kindle orange still marks a human action and nothing else. Everything else is ghost or secondary.

04

Reveal in steps

Hover shows a hint, click opens a menu, open shows a side panel that keeps your place, and only then a full page. Reading never needs a modal. Editing is asked for with a pencil or Edit, and happens in place: nothing on the page moves.

05

One way to do each thing

A screen imports parts from packages/ui; it never writes a button, card or heading class string itself. A missing part is added there first.

Shape, depth and motion

Depth says how far a thing sits from the page. The page itself is flat.

0 · Page

No border, no shadow. Sections, lists, text.

1 · Object

shadow-1, radius 8. Queue cards, figures, records.

2 · Popover

shadow-2, radius 12. Menus, tooltips, pickers.

3 · Panel

shadow-3, radius 12. Side panel, dialog, switcher.

Radius

4 badges · 8 controls and cards · 12 popovers and panels · full chips and avatars

Motion · new tokens

120 ms reveal · 160 ms popover · 200 ms panel, ease-out

Delay · new token

Tooltip waits 500 ms; hover actions show at once

Reduced motion

All of it becomes an instant swap

What adopting this changes in the repo

• ADR 0029 amends 0014: the prototype's frame stays, its bordered-card canvas gives way to the page surface (ADR 0029).

• Three motion tokens in effects.css; no colour, space or type token changes.

• New parts in packages/ui: Button, IconButton, Menu, Tooltip, Tabs, Section, ListRow, SidePanel, EmptyState.

• A lint rule: screens may not write <button class=, <h2 class= or the card frame string.

• This canvas becomes the design-system/ source AGENTS.md already reserves.

• Screens move over one page shape at a time, starting from Today.

• ADR 0026 point 3 amended by ADR 0029: the edit view keeps its address but draws the read view's frame, so editing happens in place.
