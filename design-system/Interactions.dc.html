Show more only when someone reaches for it

Four steps of disclosure. Each step keeps the person where they are a little longer before taking them somewhere new.

1 · Hover or focus

Hint

Row tint, row actions, tooltip after 500 ms. Shows what can be done, does nothing yet.

2 · Click ⋯

Menu or popover

Anchored to the control. Short choices, a picker, a quick edit. Esc or a click outside closes it.

3 · Click the row

Side panel

Opens on the right, list stays visible, the URL updates so it can be shared. Read and act here.

4 · Open full page

Page

For long work: editing a page, a run's play-by-play, a flow. Back returns to the list with the row still selected.

Touch and keyboard

No hover on a phone, so row actions are always shown there. Every hover reveal also shows on focus-within, and every menu opens from Enter and closes on Esc.

Never hide

A gate, a refusal reason, a count of what waits on you, or the one primary action. Those stay visible at rest.

How it is built

Menus and tooltips use the native popover attribute and render on the server. The side panel is a route with a ?peek= parameter, so it works without JavaScript.
