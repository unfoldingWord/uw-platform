An App For Everything in Six Pictures

A vertically and horizontally integrated operating system for the whole organization.

Six pictures that explain the platform: the problem it removes, the shape of the answer, how it follows our strategy and our money, how AI agents work inside it, and the path from here to there. Each picture stands alone; together they are the whole argument.

Figure 1

Five systems, one question

Ask "what happened under this grant, and what did it cost?" and today a person stitches exports from four or five systems by hand. On the platform, the same question is a single query over one database.

Figure 2

The spine: how the layers stack

Sources feed one canonical data layer. Agents and apps reach it only through a small set of tool verbs. Institutional memory is woven across every layer. Every "should we build X?" is answered by where X sits on this spine.

Figure 3

The Strategic Cascade becomes the schema

The vertical axis of integration: our existing cascade is not a poster on the wall — it is the database's actual shape. Money and impact attach at the portfolio tier; products sit on a crossing plane.

Figure 4

Follow the dollar

The horizontal axis of integration: one unbroken thread from a donor's gift to the field results it made possible — and back to the donor as a report.

Figure 5

Agents work here — governed

AI agents subscribe to work queues and claim cards like any team member. What they cannot do is enforced by the server, not by etiquette: the approval gate is physical, and the dangerous verbs do not exist in their toolset.

Figure 6

The path there: absorb one domain at a time

No big-bang cutover. Each system is absorbed only when its replacement is proven, and the ledger runs in parallel with Sage — reconciled to zero monthly — until we and the auditors sign off.
